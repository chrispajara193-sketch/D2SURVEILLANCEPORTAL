MISSION, VISION & GOAL

Mission:

To provide QCitizens with innovative, technology-driven, hybrid, health services through public-private alliance, functional service delivery network & sustainable financing.

Vision:

QCHD leading in the provision of accessible, available, acceptable quality health care services through public and private partnerships, inclusive of culturally sensitive & research based healthcare services, towards a Healthier Environment.

Goal:

To strengthen health-seeking behavior, increase health service utilization and improve health outcomes through enhanced delivery of comprehensive health care services.
